04 · DELIVERY

The journey through your stomach matters.

A fasted stomach is about as acidic as battery acid. Most capsules open there. Gleev's delayed-release capsule stayed intact for over an hour in acid of that strength, in laboratory testing, so its contents are carried further down before release.

LABORATORY TEST · 60:00

What this shows: two capsules in 0.1M hydrochloric acid at about pH 1, the acid strength of a fasted stomach. The standard capsule opens. Gleev's capsule is intact at 60 minutes. A laboratory test on the capsule, not a recording inside a body.

Five double-blind placebo-controlled paediatric trials in acute diarrhoea; a 137-adult randomised trial in chronic diarrhoea; a 325-patient study on antibiotic-associated diarrhoea; a 297-patient IBS-D study and a randomised crossover trial.
