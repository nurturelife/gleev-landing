Cookies and analytics

When you first visit, a banner asks whether you accept analytics cookies. Until you accept, we do not load Google Analytics and no analytics cookies are set. If you accept, Google Analytics sets cookies (named _ga and _ga_…) to measure visits. You can decline, and you can change your choice at any time with "Cookie settings" in the footer; declining removes the cookies.

Separately, your browser's local storage keeps your basket, the email address you typed, your language choice and your cookie choice, on your device only. These are needed for the site to work as you expect. You can clear them in your browser settings.
