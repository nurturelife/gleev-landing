What this website does

This site presents Gleev and lets you register your interest. If you add a plan to your basket and go through the checkout page, nothing is charged and no order is placed. We ask for your name and email so we can notify you about product availability.

What we collect

  • At the checkout page: your email address, first name, last name, the plan you selected (its name, quantity and price), your choice about receiving our newsletter and the language of the page you used.
  • If you sign up to the newsletter in the page footer: your email address and the language of the page.
  • When you sign up: a record of the sign-up, such as the time and technical details our email provider logs (for example your IP address).
  • If you accept analytics cookies: information about how you use the site, such as the pages you visit, where you click and how far you scroll, your approximate location, device and browser (see "Cookies and analytics" below).

We do not ask for card details, delivery addresses or any payment information, and we do not collect them.

Why we use it, and our legal basis

Launch notifications

If you register your interest in Gleev, we use your details to notify you about product availability. Registration does not constitute an order, and no payment is taken. Our legal basis is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time by contacting {{email}}.

Newsletter

If you subscribe to our newsletter, we use your email address to send you Gleev news, product updates and offers. Our legal basis is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in our emails.

Analytics

With your consent, we use Google Analytics and PostHog to understand how visitors use our website and improve their experience, including through heatmaps of clicks and scrolling. Our legal basis is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can change your preferences at any time through Cookie settings.

Website operation and security

We process limited technical information, such as IP addresses and browser information, to operate our website, maintain security and prevent misuse. Our legal basis is our legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) in providing a secure and functional website.

Cookies and analytics

We use optional analytics cookies to understand how visitors interact with our website. Google Analytics and PostHog are activated only when you accept analytics cookies. Until then they are not loaded and no analytics cookies are set. If you accept, Google Analytics sets cookies (named _ga and _ga_…) and PostHog stores a cookie and local-storage entries (names starting with ph_). You can withdraw your consent at any time through Cookie settings in the page footer, which will stop further analytics tracking and remove the analytics cookies from your browser.

Your browser may also store your basket, language preference and cookie preferences to ensure the website functions correctly. Any email address temporarily stored on your device should be removed after your registration is submitted.

Is providing your information required?

Providing your information is optional. Your email address, first name and surname are required if you wish to receive a launch notification. Without these details, we cannot notify you. You can browse our website without registering, subscribing to our newsletter or accepting analytics cookies.

Automated decision-making

We do not use personal data collected through this website to make solely automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects on individuals.

How long we keep it

We retain your contact details for as long as necessary to provide the communications you have requested.

If you subscribe to Gleev's marketing communications, we retain your email address for as long as you remain subscribed and continued retention is justified. You can unsubscribe at any time or request deletion of your personal data. We periodically review the information we hold and delete or anonymise data that is no longer needed.

If you register solely to receive a specific product availability notification, we retain your details for that purpose and delete them when they are no longer needed, unless you have separately subscribed to marketing communications or another lawful basis applies.

We may retain a minimal record of your opt-out to ensure you are not contacted again.

Google Analytics data is retained for 2 months. PostHog data is retained for no longer than 12 months.

Technical and security logs are retained only for as long as necessary for website operation, security and troubleshooting, in accordance with our hosting provider's applicable retention arrangements.
